LOW POLY CHINESE TOWN - FREE SAMPLE
===================================

Five models from the full pack, free to use in commercial projects.

WHAT IS INSIDE
  buildings/shop_tea     tea shop: open wooden front, counter, shelves with
                         goods, sign with Chinese characters, cloth banner on
                         a pole, hanging lanterns (4,682 triangles)
  props/stone_lion       guardian lion on a plinth (512)
  props/lantern_post     wooden post with two red lanterns (452)
  props/rice_jars        clay rice jars with red labels (2,042)
  nature/plum_tree       plum tree in blossom (632)

FORMATS
  Every model ships as .fbx, .glb, .obj (+ .mtl) and .blend.

STYLE
  Flat-shaded low poly, solid colours, no textures and no UV work.
  Every model is one object with its origin on the ground at the centre of
  its footprint. Front faces -Y in Blender, +Z in glTF and Godot.
  Real-world scale in metres. Lanterns use an emissive material called
  Lantern: turn the emission up and add bloom for night scenes.

THE FULL PACK
  47 models of an old walled Chinese town - temple hall, seven-storey
  pagoda, city gate, drum tower, teahouse, courtyard houses, shops, walls,
  bridges, pavilions, props and trees - a ready town scene, and a Blender
  add-on that generates a new walled town from any seed.
  https://eh98.itch.io/low-poly-chinese-town-47-models-town-generator

If you use these models, a rating on the itch.io page helps a lot.

LICENCE

AI DISCLOSURE
  The models are generated by a Python script that builds the geometry
  procedurally. The script was written with AI assistance. No image
  generator or 3D generator produced the meshes.
